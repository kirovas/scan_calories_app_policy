Scan Calories Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

What we collect

When you take or choose a photo of a meal, it is sent to our server and then to OpenAI's vision service solely to identify the food and estimate its nutritional content. Our server does not save a copy of your photos; they exist only for the duration of that one request.

What stays on your device

Your meal history, calorie and macronutrient goals, and language preference are stored locally on your device only. We do not have a user account system, and this data is never uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Advertising

This app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

We share photo data with OpenAI for food recognition and use Google AdMob for advertising. We do not sell your data to anyone.

Your choices

You can clear your on-device meal history and goals at any time by uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data. Camera and photo library access are only used when you choose to take or select a photo.
